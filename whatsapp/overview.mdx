Watx connects your business's own WhatsApp number through the official **Meta WhatsApp Cloud API**. Messages arrive in the shared [inbox](/inbox/overview), contacts and conversations are shared with every other channel, and everything below — templates, broadcasts, the catalogue, analytics — runs on that one connection.

## The two rules that shape everything

Every WhatsApp feature in Watx is built around the same pair of Meta rules. Read them once and the rest of this section makes sense.

<Card title="The 24-hour messaging window" icon="clock" href="/whatsapp/messaging-window">
  You can send anything you like for 24 hours after a customer's last message. Outside that window, only an approved template will send.
</Card>

<Card title="Message templates" icon="stamp" href="/whatsapp/templates">
  A template is copy Meta has reviewed and approved. It is the only way to open a conversation the customer did not start — and it can be rejected or paused by Meta at any time.
</Card>

Open **WhatsApp** in the channel rail and the panel has three groups.

| Group | Row | What it is |

| Analytics | **Orders** | [Orders placed from your catalogue](/whatsapp/catalog-and-orders) |

| Action | **Broadcasts** | [Send a template to many contacts](/whatsapp/broadcasts) |
| Action | **Campaigns** | [Campaign schedules](/whatsapp/campaigns) |
| Assets | **Templates** | [Your template library](/whatsapp/templates) |
| Assets | **WhatsApp Flows** | [Meta's native forms](/whatsapp/whatsapp-flows) |
| Assets | **Catalog** | [Products](/whatsapp/catalog-and-orders) |

<Warning>
  **Flows** and **WhatsApp Flows** are two different things and they sit in the same panel. **Flows** (Action) is the Watx chatbot builder — a graph of nodes that sends ordinary WhatsApp messages. **WhatsApp Flows** (Assets) is Meta's own product: form screens that open inside WhatsApp. This documentation always spells out "WhatsApp Flow" for Meta's version.
</Warning>

## What you need before connecting

- A Facebook account that administers your business.
- A business phone number customers will message. You can bring a new number, or connect the number already running on your WhatsApp Business app and keep its chat history.
- A payment method on your WhatsApp Business Account, added in Meta Business Settings. Meta bills you for conversations directly — Watx never sits between you and that bill. Until it is set up you can receive messages, but messages your business starts will fail.

Full walkthrough: [Connect your number](/whatsapp/connect).

## What else uses the connection

- The [inbox composer](/inbox/sending) — text, media, voice notes, reactions, quoted replies, templates, product cards.
- [Automations](/automations/overview) and [flows](/flows/overview), which send through the same number and obey the same window.
- [Payment links](/integrations/razorpay) and [sales documents](/integrations/sales-documents-sending), which fall back to a template when the window is closed.
- [AI agents](/ai-agents/overview), which reply on WhatsApp like any other channel.

If messages are not arriving or not sending, start with [WhatsApp delivery troubleshooting](/troubleshooting/whatsapp).
